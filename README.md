# Screens, Sleep & Circadian Health: A Learning Module for Emerging Adults

An interactive, self-paced (~25-minute) digital wellness learning module designed for emerging adults (ages 18–29).

## 🌐 Live Learning Module
**[Launch the Interactive Module](https://techwriter23.github.io/Screens_Sleep_Learning_Module/)**

## 🎯 Learning Goals
Learners will be able to explain evening screen light impacts, understand emerging adult risk factors, compare four evidence-based strategies, and adapt advice for diverse cultural and family contexts.

## 📚 Module Sections
* **Why Emerging Adults?** — Developmental context, peer habits, and FOMO.
* **The Science: From Screen to Sleep** — Blue light, ipRGC pathways, and melatonin suppression.
* **Culture & Context** — Adapting recommendations for varied household and economic realities.
* **Building the Skills** — SEL competencies applied to screen habits.
* **Four Evidence-Based Strategies** — Spectral tuning, bedroom sanctuary, disconnection windows, and chronotherapy.
* **Check Your Understanding & References** — Interactive practice and citations.
